Cities Run Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Controller: Gicavi · Contact: gicavigames@gmail.com

1. About the app

Cities Run is a mobile game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

2. Data we collect

  • No user account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or password.
  • We do not collect our own personal data. We run no servers that receive your gameplay data.
  • Saved games stay on your device. Your progress (coins, gems, items, settings, records, missions) is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases it.
  • Permissions. Internet and network state (to load ads), and Google's advertising ID permission (AD_ID) added by the ads SDK. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded ads: they only appear when you tap to watch one (e.g. to revive, double a reward or get an extra chest). There are no interstitial ads or banners. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and technical device data to serve and measure ads, and may personalize them depending on your consent and local law. More information: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Consent. On startup the app uses Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform, UMP), which is only shown where the law requires it (European Economic Area and United Kingdom). You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy and ads (if no form is needed in your region, the app tells you). If you do not consent, no ads are requested or they are shown with the setup Google allows; the game works the same. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Purchases

The game currently offers no real-money purchases. The in-game shop uses only in-game coins and gems earned by playing. If purchases are added, this policy will be updated.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, has not been flagged as child-directed in the ads configuration and does not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

6. Security and retention

Game data lives only on your device, under Android's own protections. We keep no data about you on servers.

7. Your rights

Since we do not process our own personal data, there is nothing for us to access or delete; your progress is erased by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android. To request information or deletion of any data you believe we hold about you, write to us at gicavigames@gmail.com. For ad-related data, you can also exercise your rights with Google.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
